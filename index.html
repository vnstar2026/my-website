Hi, I'm Jennie Nguyen - a biomedical student researcher, founder, and curious problem-solver driven by one question: how can science make a difference beyond the laboratory? From exploring computational biology and neuroscience to founding Lotus Biomed Vanguard, I enjoy connecting research, technology, and human experiences to tackle overlooked challenges. I believe curiosity should lead to action, and that the most meaningful discoveries are those that make knowledge accessible to everyone.
