A person is not
the same as a profile.

The file looks straightforward at first glance. Student. Researcher. Builder. A list of interests that seems to explain the person behind them.

But the record is incomplete. The real story is hidden in the questions, choices, and revisions between those labels.

Investigator's note

These labels describe what the subject does. They do not yet explain why she does it, what changes her mind, or what happens when the evidence disagrees with her expectations.
